Support

Last updated: 2026-10-18

Contact

For bugs, questions, reports of inappropriate stages, or data deletion requests, email support@danmaku-maker.example. Including the 5-character stage ID helps us respond faster.

FAQ

  • How do I restore my purchase? Tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen. You need the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
  • I found an inappropriate stage. Use "Report" on the stage detail screen. You can also "Block author" to hide their stages.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Settings → Delete account in the App.
  • Do I need to sign in to publish? Yes. Playing needs no sign-in; publishing, liking, and reporting require Sign in with Apple.
  • I can't clear my own stage. You must clear your stage in a test play before you can publish it.
